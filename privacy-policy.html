Toss to PDF

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version: your files never leave your device. The developer never sees them, never stores them, and never wants them.

Your Files Stay on Your Device

All file conversion happens entirely on your Mac or iPhone. Your files are never uploaded, transmitted, or sent to any server. The developer has no ability to access, read, copy, or receive any file you process with this app — not even temporarily.

No Data Collection

Toss to PDF does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing about how you use the app is recorded or transmitted.

Crash Reports

If the app crashes, Apple's standard crash reporting system may send a diagnostic report to the developer. This report contains only technical information about the crash — device type, operating system version, and a stack trace — and never includes any of your files, file names, or file contents.

Pro Subscription

The Toss to PDF Pro subscription is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives only aggregate, anonymized sales information from Apple. Your payment details and Apple ID are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and are never shared with the developer.

Third-Party Services

Toss to PDF does not integrate with any third-party advertising, analytics, or data-broker services.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please email zacb@alliancearch.com.
